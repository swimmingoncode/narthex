# Idea: a reproducible "engineering lead + AI architect" workflow

Pablo, 2026-10-03, captured so it doesn't derail the aledgent session.

## The flow, as practised on aledgent

1. **Idea:** a product or feature idea, stated by the lead.
2. **Investigate:** research agents read the code, papers and data, and write sourced reports (file:line, run ids).
3. **Spec:** an initial SPEC (normative requirements, invariants, open questions) and an OVERVIEW.
4. **Discuss:** meetings with notes, and rulings captured as data, never only in chat.
5. **Decide by form:** a web form (an artifact with a db) holds every open question:
   - grouped into sections and sub-tabs;
   - open questions first, defaults applied for the rest;
   - concise copy;
   - push notifications on updates;
   - answers read back by the AI directly, and an Outbox of answered questions with the original question.
6. **Plan:** a PLAN with DECs, features, tasks and milestones.
7. **Execute:** agents in parallel, one per feature, with PRs merged when CI is green. They follow house rules: never override signing, no pushes to protected repos.
8. **Amend:** numbered amendments when reality changes the spec.
9. **Propose upward:** upstream proposals (e.g. to VOS or CipherClerk) with measurements.
10. **Report:** concise updates, long content in pages, and chat of 5 lines or fewer.

## Reusable pieces that already exist

- The form generator: `handovers/pending/build.py`, `artifact_template.html` and `rewrites.json`. Published as a private page, with answers in db collection `responses`.
- House rules for agents: aledgent's `IMPL-BRIEF.md`, in a local checkout.
- Memory feedback files: `feedback_long_content_in_files`, `feedback_list_pending_questions_explicitly`, `feedback_commit_identity`, `reference_decisions_artifact`.
- Example outputs: aledgent `SPEC.md`, `PLAN.md`, `amendments/`, `features/`, `proposals/`.

## Questions to explore

- **Skills vs. plugin vs. harness:** probably a plugin bundling several skills (intake, research, spec, decision-form, plan, dispatch, amend, propose-upstream, report) plus the form template, not a custom harness.
- **Is the decision form a product on its own?** A "decision inbox and outbox for AI-led engineering": structured questions, defaults, audit trail, two-way sync with the agent.
- **What must stay human:** product rulings, security trade-offs, outward actions.
