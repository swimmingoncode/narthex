# Codenames (brainstorm, 2026-10-17)

"SDD Studio" is dropped (ruling R1). A codename is used until a final name is found. The premise is
Prometheus: fire for mankind, here the ability to produce software at production scale. That means a senior
architect in every agent, guiding people from intent to production. No name below has been checked against
trademarks, domains, npm or GitHub.

| Codename | Meaning | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Narthex** | The hollow giant-fennel stalk in which Prometheus carried fire from the gods to mankind | The vessel, not the fire: the method carries the capability to people. Rare, memorable, searchable. My pick for the codename |
| **Forethought** | The literal meaning of "Prometheus" (pro-mētheus) | Says what the product does: think before building. A common English word, so weak as a trademark, fine as a codename |
| **Pyrphoros** | "Fire-bearer", an epithet of Prometheus | Precise, but hard to spell and pronounce |
| **Daedalus** | The master craftsman and architect of myth | Fits the architect role. Widely used already (software and games) |
| **Ember** | What fire travels as | Friendly, but taken by a well-known JavaScript framework |
| **Kindle** | To start a fire | Taken (Amazon) |
| **Hearth** | Where fire is kept and shared | Warm and team-flavoured; less about engineering |
| **Keystone** | The stone that holds an arch together | An architecture metaphor; very common name |
| **Blueprint** | The architect's plan | Generic, heavily used |
| **Torch** | Fire handed on, from one person to the next | Collides with PyTorch's `torch` |

Recommendation: **Narthex** as the codename now. It carries the premise without overpromising, and it is
unusual enough not to collide in conversation. Choose the final name later, after a trademark and domain search,
and with the book's title in mind.

## Chosen: Narthex (Pablo, 2026-10-17)

Pablo adopted **Narthex** as the codename for both of its meanings:
- **the vessel:** the fennel stalk that carried Prometheus's fire to mankind;
- **the church narthex:** the entrance hall where catechumens stood. They had been converted but not yet
  baptised. Here they are people turned toward software engineering who have not yet graduated.

The final name stays open.
