# Name alternatives to "Narthex" (brainstorm and screen, 2026-10-17)

[Narthex](2026-10-17-codenames.md) screened **medium** risk ([name search](2026-10-17-name-search.md)): a Claude Code
MCP project, a church SaaS and a Uruguayan BI vendor share the name, and every exact-match domain is held. This note
lists alternatives, screens the strongest, and ranks five. A book on the method will share the brand family, so each
name is also read as a book title. Names must work in English and Spanish.

**This is not legal advice.** Everything below is a desk screen with free tools; a lawyer's clearance search is needed
before any name is adopted.

## 3. Ranking

1. **Ferula** (Low to medium). The only name in the screen with free registries, a free .dev and no software product
   found. It is the Latin name of the plant Narthex was named after, so the story carries over. Headline risk: in
   Spanish *férula* means a ruler's rod and "bajo la férula" means under someone's thumb, the opposite of the
   mentoring tone.
2. **Calamus / Cálamo** (Medium). The best idea: the stalk that carried fire is also the pen that writes the spec, and
   the book could be "The Reed". Registries are mostly free and .dev is open. Headline risk: calamus.ai, a PyPI
   library and a legacy DTP product; a recent .com owner.
3. **Pronoia** (Medium to high). Best on meaning (forethought is what "Prometheus" says) and free on all registries.
   Headline risk: an Arabic LLM and an agentic research repo already use it in AI, and the .dev is held.
4. **Fennex** (Provisional, registry screen only). Clean registries and brandable, but the Prometheus link is hidden
   and the .com and .dev are held. Needs a mark and product search before it counts.
5. **Lintel** (High). Good threshold-plus-architecture meaning and fine in Spanish through *dintel*. Headline risk:
   an open-source architecture linter for AI coding agents with the same name, and all three registries taken.

Dropped: Vitruvius (a software mark filed for an "AI architect"), Plumbline (two agentic-architecture projects),
Portico and Pharos (crowded), Cimbra (sounds like Zimbra), Tekton (an existing CI project).

**Narthex re-rated.** On fit it would rank second, because it holds both meanings (the vessel and the church hall)
and no candidate beats that. On clearance it sits at medium, between Calamus and Pronoia, with a worse collision
than either: an MCP server of the same name in the Claude Code ecosystem, plus a church SaaS and a BI vendor.
Ferula is the safest swap; Calamus is the best story if its AI-site and DTP overlaps clear.

**Not legal advice.** Next steps: run USPTO, EUIPO, TMview and WIPO searches by hand (classes 9, 41, 42) for the top
three, check the Spanish-language market (Spain, Colombia, Mexico), and ask a trademark lawyer for clearance.
